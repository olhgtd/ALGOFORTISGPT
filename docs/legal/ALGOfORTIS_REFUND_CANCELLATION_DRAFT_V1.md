## 2. Future Commercial Release Provisions (Post-Beta)

When AlgoFortis transitions to commercial paid licensing, the following principles shall govern cancellations and refunds:

### A. Subscription Term & Cancellation
- **Self-Service / Operator Cancellation:** Users may cancel upcoming billing renewals at any time prior to the next billing cycle.
- **Access Duration:** Upon cancellation, access to premium backtesting datasets and cloud synchronization (if applicable) shall remain active through the end of the currently paid billing period.

### B. Digital Software & Refund Policy
- **Local Software Nature:** Because AlgoFortis is downloadable desktop software running locally on user hardware with access to full historical datasets, refunds following activation are generally restricted to prevent abuse, subject to applicable Indian consumer protection laws.
- **Trial / Evaluation Window:** The Company will provide a free trial or verified evaluation period before commercial payment to ensure software compatibility with user hardware.
- **Exceptional Refunds:** Full or partial refunds may be issued at the Company's sole discretion in instances of documented technical incompatibilities where the Software cannot be made operational on a supported Windows workstation despite timely support escalation.
